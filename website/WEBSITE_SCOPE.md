# DineCircle Public Website: Scope & Purpose

The DineCircle website should be a marketing, business promotion, and app download platform, not a web version of the Customer App or Partner App.

Its main goals are to attract customers, promote dining experiences, encourage mobile app downloads, and onboard restaurant partners.

## 1. Core Website Pages

| Page               | Purpose                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Home               | Introduce DineCircle, showcase its value, and drive app installs         |
| How It Works       | Explain discovering restaurants, deals, hosting, and joining experiences |
| Dining Experiences | Promote social dining, community gatherings, and hosts                   |
| For Restaurants    | Explain partner benefits, pricing, and business growth                   |
| Become a Partner   | Restaurant application and onboarding                                    |
| For Hosts          | Explain professional accounts and Host Studio                            |
| Download App       | Android/iOS availability, download links, and QR code                    |
| About Us           | Brand story, mission, and vision                                         |
| Contact & Support  | Business inquiries, support, and FAQs                                     |
| Legal              | Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Community Guidelines               |

## 3. Website In Scope vs. Out of Scope

| In Scope                            | Out of Scope                     |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Brand promotion and SEO             | Full customer web application    |
| App download and installation links | Web-based Host Studio            |
| Restaurant partner applications     | Partner App management dashboard |
| Partner pricing and benefits        | Online food ordering             |
| Host Studio promotion               | Web-based experience creation    |
| App features and screenshots        | Online checkout and payments     |
| Contact and support                 | Social media feeds and messaging |
| Legal and policy pages              | Restaurant POS and inventory     |

## 4. Website Conversion Goals

| Audience    | Action              | Role                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Customers   | Download DineCircle | Primary conversion goal              |
| Restaurants | Apply as Partner    | Primary business revenue goal        |
| Creators    | Become a Host       | Grow the dining experience ecosystem |

## 5. Design Direction

Use a premium, modern, lifestyle-focused website with warm orange branding, high-quality restaurant photography, real dining experiences, app screenshots, elegant typography, and clear calls to action.

The website should feel like a consumer lifestyle brand rather than an enterprise software dashboard.

Final recommendation: Keep three distinct products:

- Public Website: Marketing, app downloads, and partner applications.
- Customer & Partner Mobile Apps: Actual customer and restaurant functionality.
- Private Admin Panel: Platform management, approvals, subscriptions, and moderation.

This separation keeps DineCircle focused, easy to navigate, and easier to maintain.
