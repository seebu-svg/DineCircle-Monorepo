For hosts and creators

Turn what you know about food into a table

Hosts plan the evening: the venue, the menu, who comes and how the table runs. DineCircle brings the guests and holds the seats. Host accounts are free, and everything is planned inside the app.

Get the app See the three steps

What a host does

You bring the people. We bring the seats.

A host is a food creator, a critic, a community organiser or simply the person everyone asks where to eat. You choose the format; the venue serves the meal.

  • Plan the experience: date, time, seats, menu style and what the evening is about
  • Set who it is for: a first-in-city crowd, a brunch regulars table, a grill night
  • Track seats from Host Studio, so you always know how full the table is
  • Meet your guests at the venue on the night and run the room

Getting started

Three steps, and no approval queue

Hosting is self-service inside the app. There is no application form, no interview and no fee, because the experience is booked and served by the venue.

  1. 01

    Get the app

    Download DineCircle and create your account. It is the same app diners use, so your bookings and your hosted tables live in one place.

  2. 02

    Switch to Professional

    In account settings, change your account type to Professional. That promotes you to host and unlocks Host Studio on the same sign-in.

  3. 03

    Plan and publish

    In Host Studio, set the venue, the date, the seats and the format, then publish. Guests join free and pay the venue for their own meal.

Switching back to a personal account is available whenever you have no upcoming experience still on the calendar, so guests are never left holding a seat that no longer exists.

Community and safety

Hosted tables carry a responsibility

Guests are meeting people their own table recommended them. These are the notes we ask every host to keep to.

Be honest about the format

Say who the table is for and what happens on the night. A guest who arrives expecting a quiet dinner to a loud grill night did not choose wrong.

Confirm with the venue

Agree the menu, the seating and the cancellation terms with the restaurant before you publish. The venue is the host of the room; you are the host of the table.

Respect the guests

No pressure to buy anything, no recruitment from the table, and no sharing a guest contact with anyone. Report a problem and the listing is reviewed.

Full expectations live in the community guidelines.

FAQs

Host questions

Still unsure? Ask the team before you publish anything.

How do I become a host?

Create a DineCircle account, open account settings and switch to a Professional account. That unlocks Host Studio in the app, where you plan and publish your experiences.

Does hosting cost anything?

No. Host accounts are free, there is no listing fee and no commission. You agree the menu and format with the venue, and guests settle their own meals at the table.
