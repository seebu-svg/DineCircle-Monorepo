Activation

From application to live listing

Four steps, and a real person verifies the payment at the end of them. There is no self-serve activation, because there is no online card payment.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Send the venue name, the area and a contact who can answer questions about the menu. It takes about two minutes.

  2. 02

    Verify

    We confirm the venue, the menu and who is authorised to represent it. If something is unclear we reply with a question rather than moving on.

  3. 03

    Activate

    Send the subscription by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Your account is switched to active once the payment is verified.

  4. 04

    Publish

    Your menu, QR code, deals and reservation settings go live. Updates after that are yours to make from the Partner app.

What we do not do

Clear boundaries, on purpose

DineCircle is a discovery and booking platform. It is not a delivery service and not a point-of-sale system, so it will not ask to sit inside your operation.

No online ordering

Guests order at the table, in your restaurant, the way your kitchen already works.

No payments taken

Bills are settled between the guest and the venue. DineCircle does not process or hold guest money.

No POS integration

No new hardware, no till replacement and no training day for your floor team.

FAQs

Partner questions

Prefer to write first? Email partners@dinecircle.app.

What does a partnership cost?

PKR 5,000 per month or PKR 50,000 per year, covering every venue feature: menu, QR menu, deals, reservations, experiences and reviews. There is no per-booking charge on top.

Do you take commission on bookings?

No. DineCircle charges a flat subscription and never a share of your covers or bills. You keep the reservation details and settle nothing with us per booking.

How does activation work?

After your application we verify the venue, send the invoice, and activate once payment arrives by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. A person does this, so allow one working day for each step.

Do we need a payment gateway?

No. DineCircle does not take card details and does not route payments. Guests pay you directly at the table, so there is no gateway, reconciliation or payout setup on your side.
