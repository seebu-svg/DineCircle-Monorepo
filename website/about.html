About DineCircle

Great food. Shared moments.

DineCircle is a Karachi-first discovery platform for restaurants, cafés and the people who bring tables together. Free for diners, honest about menus, and built around paying the venue directly.

Our story

A simpler way to decide where to eat

Restaurant discovery in Karachi usually means scrolling through photos, guessing at prices and calling three numbers to find a table. DineCircle puts the useful parts in one place: the full menu with PKR prices, current deals with their terms, real opening hours and a way to join a table with other people.

We are not a delivery app, a payment gateway or a social network. We do not take a cut of your bill. Restaurants list their menus and offers; hosts plan experiences; diners show up and pay the venue. That is the whole loop.

What we stand for

Three rules we do not bend

Free for diners

No booking fee, no subscription and no commission on your bill. If a venue charges for food, that is the only charge you see.

Real menus, real prices

Every dish is listed by the venue in PKR. No bait pricing, no vague market price and no DineCircle markup.

Local tables

FAQs

Questions, answered

Still stuck? Email hello@dinecircle.app and we will reply within one working day.

Is DineCircle free?

Yes. Browsing, booking tables and joining dining experiences are free for diners. DineCircle takes no booking commission. You pay the restaurant for your food and drinks.

Do I need the app to book a table?

Table bookings and experience seats are confirmed in the DineCircle app. This website is for discovery: menus, deals, venue details and host profiles. Download links are opening in phases across Karachi.

How do deals work?

Find an offer on the Deals page or the venue page, show it to your server before ordering, and the venue applies it to the bill. Some offers need a code; others just need you to ask. Each deal lists its own terms.

What does joining an experience cost?

Joining is free. Each experience shows an estimated dining cost per person, which you pay directly to the venue on the day. There is no online checkout and no DineCircle fee.

Are the menus and prices accurate?

Menus, prices and opening hours are supplied by each venue and can change with the season. This site shows preview content for the Karachi pilot. Confirm details with the restaurant before you order.

Which cities do you cover?

Karachi is live. Lahore and Islamabad are next, with the same model: free for diners, subscription-based for partners and zero booking commission.
