Privacy Policy

DineCircle collects the minimum information needed to help you discover restaurants, book tables and join dining experiences. This policy explains what we hold and why.

Who we are

DineCircle is a restaurant discovery platform based in Karachi, Pakistan. This policy explains how we handle personal information on the DineCircle website and in the DineCircle customer and partner apps.

For privacy questions, contact hello@dinecircle.app.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account details you provide in the app, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Booking and experience details, including the venue, date, party size and any dietary notes you choose to share.
  • Deal redemptions recorded when a venue applies an offer.
  • Support messages you send to us.
  • Website form submissions, kept so an enquiry can be answered and tracked. A partner application stores the venue name, your contact name, email, phone, area and your message. A launch-invite entry stores your email and the platform you chose.
  • Technical information such as device type, browser, approximate location derived from your IP address, and pages viewed.

This marketing website does not create accounts and does not process payments.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure your account and confirm bookings or experience seats.
  • Share the details a venue needs to host you, such as the name on the booking and party size.
  • Apply and track deals and respond to support requests.
  • Understand which pages and venues are useful so we can improve the product.
  • Send service messages, and marketing messages only where you have opted in.
  • Meet legal obligations and prevent misuse or fraud.

Cookies and local storage

This website stores your selected city in your browser's local storage so the restaurant filters keep it on your next visit. We do not use advertising cookies on this site.

If we add analytics, it will be configured to avoid identifying individual visitors. You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings.

When we share information

We share information with:

  • Venues and hosts, limited to what is needed to honour a booking or experience.
  • Service providers that host our infrastructure, send email or provide analytics, under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users, venues or DineCircle.

We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep information

Account and booking records are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal, accounting and dispute-resolution needs. Support messages are kept for up to 24 months. Aggregated analytics that cannot identify you may be kept longer.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw marketing consent, by emailing hello@dinecircle.app. We will respond within a reasonable period. Deleting your account removes your profile; limited booking records may be retained where the law requires it.

Website enquiry records are deleted once the enquiry is closed and a venue or guest relationship exists, or on request if it does not.

Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit and least-privilege access for staff and partners. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required.

Children

DineCircle is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. The effective date at the top of this page will change, and material updates will be announced in the app or by email where appropriate.

Related: the terms of service and the community guidelines. To exercise a right, use the contact page and choose Privacy or my account.
